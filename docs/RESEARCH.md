# Research and interpretation

Research informs design, not validation of the full simulation. Population, social behavior and time are simplified. Market-triggered behavior is fictional.

## Social life
- [Population density and social pathology](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/population-density-and-social-patho/)
- [Death squared](https://doi.org/10.1177/00359157730661P202)
- [Empathy and pro-social behavior in rats](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1210789)
- [Open-access prosocial research](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3760221/)

Crowding does not establish a universal inevitable social outcome. Habitat, resource access, individual differences and experimental context matter.

## Locomotion
- Estimation of skeletal kinematics in freely moving rodents, Nature Methods (2022).
- Comprehensive dynamic and kinematic analysis of the rodent hindlimb during over ground walking, Scientific Reports (2022).
- [RatWalker protocol](https://www.jove.com/v/62002/applying-ratwalker-system-for-gait-analysis-genetic-rat-model)
- [3D kinematic gait analysis](https://www.jove.com/v/59612/3d-kinematic-gait-analysis-for-preclinical-studies-in-rodents)

Adaptations include staggered support phases, joint constraints, torso motion and delayed secondary movement. The animation is an artistic approximation, not a reconstruction of measured kinematics.
