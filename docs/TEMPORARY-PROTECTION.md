# Temporary assisted care
An operator may activate a bounded careProtection.until Unix timestamp in milliseconds. The worker supplies authoritative simulation timestamps to ticks. During the interval, simulated deaths and new conceptions are suspended, biological time advances at 1% speed, and active care replenishes food/water/energy, maintains comfortable warmth, treats injuries and lowers individual stress. Existing pregnancies are preserved and advance slowly. Normal rules resume automatically at the deadline, including age-related mortality; this is not permanent immortality.
A visible assisted-care notice identifies the intervention. Alert thresholds are unchanged: actual occupancy/crowding can still warn. Trade ingestion, financial ledgers, names, ownership and history remain intact. This cannot guarantee infrastructure uptime.
